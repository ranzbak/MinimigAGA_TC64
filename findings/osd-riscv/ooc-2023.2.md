# OOC size and timing of the three VexOsd cores (Vivado 2023.2)

Production tool Vivado 2023.2, `xc7a100tfgg676-2`, out of context, registered-I/O
wrapper, period 8.815 ns (clk_114), flow `tools/vivado/ooc_hostcpu` (synth
`-flatten_hierarchy rebuilt`, opt, place, phys_opt, route). Cores as committed
in `rtl/host/vexriscv/` (RV32IMC, 4 KB I$, `mtvec` RW, both catch flags on:
misaligned load/store trap and illegal-instruction trap). LUT is the total of
the `u_core` row in `util_hier.rpt` (includes LUTRAM and SRL).

| Core | LUT | LUTRAM | SRL | FF | RAMB36 / RAMB18 | DSP | WNS (ns) | est Fmax MHz | Gate | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `VexOsd` (no debug) | 1367 | 0 | 0 | 964 | 1 / 3 | 4 | +1.428 | 135 | <= 1450 LUT, >= +1.0 ns | PASS |
| `VexOsd_DMT` (DM, BSCANE2 tunnel ports) | 1846 | 22 | 3 | 1645 | 1 / 3 | 4 | +1.135 | 130 | <= 1950 LUT, >= +0.9 ns | PASS |
| `VexOsd_DMJ` (DM, own TAP) | 1830 | 22 | 0 | 1690 | 1 / 3 | 4 | +1.344 | 134 | <= 1950 LUT, >= +0.9 ns | PASS |

`tck` (50 ns, asynchronous to `clk`) has +46.2 ns setup slack in `VexOsd_DMT`.

## Against the 2026.1 survey (`tiny-cores.md`)

| Run | Tool | LUT | FF | RAMB36/18 | DSP | WNS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| VexRiscv IMC + 4 KB I$ + DM (survey, `VexTiny_IMC_IC4K_DM`) | 2026.1 | 1742 | 1639 | 1 / 3 | 4 | +1.104 |
| `VexOsd_DMT` | 2023.2 | 1846 | 1645 | 1 / 3 | 4 | +1.135 |
| `VexOsd_DMJ` | 2023.2 | 1830 | 1690 | 1 / 3 | 4 | +1.344 |

The OSD cores cost about +90..105 LUT over the survey core. Configuration
(trap catches, RW `mtvec`, cache options) and tool version both differ, so the
delta is not split further.
Both catch flags are on in every row; no gate failed, so the catch-flags-off
runs were not needed.

## Parser validation

4 and WNS +1.428; `util_hier.rpt` has `u_core | VexOsd | 1367 | 1367 | 0 | 0 |
964 | 1 | 3 | 4` and `timing.rpt` has clk WNS 1.428 (`summary.txt` wns=1.428).
