The OSD cores cost about +90..105 LUT over the survey core. Configuration
(trap catches, RW `mtvec`, cache options) and tool version both differ, so the
delta is not split further.
Both catch flags are on in every row; no gate failed, so the catch-flags-off
runs were not needed.

`rv1` (Task 14): the BSCANE2 TCK output (`genrv.myrv/g_core.u_bscan/u_bscane2/TCK`) is a
50 ns clock `tck`, asynchronous to `clk`; `tck` WNS +46.297 ns, WHS +0.121 ns. Primitives:
1 BSCANE2, 1 BUFG (BUFGCTRL 1/32). rv1 vs rv0: +499 LUT, +680 FF; vs rv2 +13 LUT, -45 FF.
`util_hier` shows 24 LUT under `g_core.u_bscan`: they are the DTM's `dmi`/`dtmcs` shift-register
enables (`logic_jtagLogic_*_store[*]_i_*`, inputs `sel`/`update`/`shift`) that
`-flatten_hierarchy rebuilt` pushed into that level, not logic of the wrapper (`rv_bscane2`
is only the two primitives); `VexOsd_DMT` itself 1868 LUT / 1642 FF. Worst `clk` paths as
rv2: `decode_to_execute_INSTRUCTION_reg[7]` -> `RegFilePlugin_regFile_reg_2/ADDRARDADDR[5]`,

`rv0` breakdown (util_hier): `rv_hostcpu` 1450 LUT / 1071 FF, of which `VexOsd` core
1400 (I$ 312) / 962 and the CPU block itself 50 / 109; boot ROM 18 LUT; hostcache 65 / 75
+ 1 RAMB36; bridge top 106 FF.

`hw_req_reg` and `wr_reg` cells (`get_cells -hier -filter {NAME =~ ...}`): `*hw_req_reg*` = 1

Both XDC cell names the board constraints use exist.

### Worst paths

All runs: the worst `clk` setup paths are inside the CPUs, not on the bus interface.
- `832`: `alu_d1_reg[31]` -> `alu/mulresult_reg/PCIN` (DSP), +2.252.
- `rv0` (top 5, unique pins): `decode_to_execute_RS2_reg[2]` -> `RegFilePlugin_regFile_reg_{1,2}/ADDRARDADDR[5..9]`
  (register-file BRAM address), +1.680, +1.703, +1.705, +1.840, +1.883.
- `rv2`: `decode_to_execute_INSTRUCTION_reg[11]` -> `RegFilePlugin_regFile_reg_2/ADDRARDADDR[5]`, +0.872
  (then +0.911, +0.967 same endpoints).

The Task 4 item (in `rv_hostcpu`, `iBus_cmd_ready` and the output-register inputs depend
combinationally on `dBus_cmd_valid`), measured on the routed `rv0` netlist:
- through `d_fire` (= `~busy & dBus_cmd_valid`, drives the output-register CEs):
  `u_core/decode_to_execute_RS2_reg[0]` -> `genrv.myrv/q_reg[*]/CE`, 6 logic levels
  (CARRY4 + 5 LUT6), slack **+3.262 ns**.
- through the core's `dBus_cmd_valid` net into the I$: `decode_to_execute_RS2_reg[0]` ->
  `IBusCachedPlugin_cache/lineLoader_cmdSent_reg/D`, 7 levels, slack **+3.877 ns**.
Neither is critical (the address generation in the core is the long part, and the
`dBus_cmd_valid` decode adds little); worst slack on that structure is 1.6 ns better
than the core's own register-file path.

### Boot ROM inference

Vivado infers BRAM for both ROMs ("recognized as a true dual port RAM template" for
`OSDBoot_832_ROM:/ram_reg` and `OSDBoot_rv_ROM:/ram_reg`) in spite of the two processes
driving `ram` in the template. RV ROM: 2 RAMB36 + 18 LUT of glue. Tiles: 832 total 3 RAMB36
(ROM 2, hostcache 1; the 832 core uses none); rv0 total 4 RAMB36 + 3 RAMB18 (ROM 2, hostcache 1,
core 1 RAMB36 + 3 RAMB18 = 2 register-file halves + I$ tag/data).
